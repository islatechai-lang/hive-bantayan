Build a complete production-ready Android application called "Bantayan Hub".

PURPOSE

A local marketplace app exclusively for Bantayan Island.

The platform allows:

* Customers to discover and order products from local businesses.
* Business owners to create their own storefronts.
* Admins to manage the entire platform.

The application should feel modern, premium, fast, and simple.
